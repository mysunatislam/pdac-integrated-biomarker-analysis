# Figure Captions

The manuscript-ready figures below are available in both `.png` and `.pdf` formats under `results/figures/manuscript/`.

## Figure 1

`Figure_1_integrated_workflow`

Integrated multi-cohort analytical workflow covering tissue differential expression, malignant-switch filtering, spatial validation, PPI prioritization, EV/plasma miRNA analysis, cross-study assessment, and curated miRNA-hub-gene evidence.

## Figure 2

`Figure_2_differential_expression_malignant_switch`

Differential-expression analysis and malignant-switch gene-selection strategy in `GSE143754`, including PCA and volcano plots for CP versus normal, PDAC versus CP, and PDAC versus normal.

## Figure 3

`Figure_3_spatial_validation_stage_association`

Spatial validation and stage-association analysis of malignant-switch genes in `GSE208536`, showing the 48 verified GeoMx regions, 80 represented genes, 59 ANOVA stage-associated genes, and nine genes with both ANOVA and ordinal Spearman support.

## Figure 4

`Figure_4_functional_enrichment_ppi_prioritization`

Functional enrichment and PPI-network prioritization of spatially validated malignant-switch genes, including GO/KEGG enrichment, MCODE modules, and Cytoscape hub ranking.

## Figure 5

`Figure_5_shared_stage_associated_hub_genes`

Overlap of 59 spatial stage-associated genes with 18 PPI-derived hub genes, yielding 12 shared hub genes, plus row-scaled spatial expression heatmap.

## Figure 6

`Figure_6_ev_mirna_panel_performance`

EV-miRNA candidate prioritization and diagnostic performance of the six-miRNA PDAC panel, including candidate discovery, validation assessment, expression distributions, and ROC curves.

## Figure 7

`Figure_7_secondary_gse268771_assessment`

Secondary exploratory assessment of candidate-miRNA reproducibility and six-miRNA panel transferability in `GSE268771`.

## Supplementary Figure S1

`Supplementary_Figure_S1_curated_mirna_hub_network`

Curated high-throughput associations between final-panel miRNAs and shared stage-associated PPI hub genes.
